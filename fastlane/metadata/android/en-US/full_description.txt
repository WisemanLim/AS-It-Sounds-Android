As It Sounds transcribes audio and video entirely on your device — no cloud upload, full privacy, works in airplane mode.

• On-device speech recognition (whisper.cpp), 6+ languages
• Korean phonetic transliteration shown alongside the original
• On-device Korean translation (ML Kit), or MyMemory / LibreTranslate / DeepL
• 3-line segment view: original / Hangul pronunciation / Korean translation
• Tap a segment to seek, A-B repeat, 0.5–2× speed, ±5s
• Export SRT / VTT / TXT
• Import via file picker, photo picker, or share sheet

All processing happens on-device. No data is collected or shared.
